Cao ốc Văn phòng HMCIC

Cao ốc Văn phòng HMCIC is a building in District 1, Ho Chi Minh City, which is located on Trần Hưng Đạo. Cao ốc Văn phòng HMCIC is situated near the hospital Bệnh viện Đa khoa Tân Định - Cơ sở 2, as well as near the theater building Nhà hát Cải lương Trần Hữu Trang.
