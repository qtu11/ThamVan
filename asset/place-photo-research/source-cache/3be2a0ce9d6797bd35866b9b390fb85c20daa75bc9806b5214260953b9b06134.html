The 16 best rooftop bars and sky bars in Saigon (Ho Chi Minh City), 2026

16 rooftop bars and sky bars in Saigon, ranked by Google Maps review count, from a lounge on floors 75–76 of Landmark 81, the highest on this list, to heritage hotel terraces around the Opera House where a band plays every night, plus a poolside bar that opens in the morning. Each entry gives the floor, the view, the hours, the closing day and the kind of evening it suits.

Updated 26 Oct 2026 · 16 venues · ranked by number of Google Maps reviews

Search for the best rooftop bar in Ho Chi Minh City and you get dozens of names, but they differ in three ways that matter: how high they are, which way they face and how late they stay open. It helps to think of this list in three bands. At the top are the true sky bars: Blank Sky Lounge on floors 75–76 of Landmark 81 in Binh Thanh, Song Bar on the 40th floor of the Hilton by the river, Chill Skybar on the AB Tower on Le Lai and Kén Skybar in the Riverfront tower on Ton Duc Thang, both on floor 26, Social Club on floor 24 of Hôtel des Arts, and Shri and Hai Bar, both on floor 23. In the middle sit Rooftop Grand Lounge on floor 20, Zion on floor 14 of a Ham Nghi tower, Papaya Papa on floor 13 on Hai Ba Trung and Twilight on floor 12 on Ly Tu Trong. Lowest, and closest to street life, are Banana Mama on floor 10 next to Bui Vien, SolLuna, also on floor 10, beside the pool of the Icon Saigon hotel, Saigon Saigon on the ninth floor of the Caravelle, M Bar on floor 8 of the Majestic and the Rex Rooftop Garden Bar, which guides place on floor 5.

Something most guides skip: Saigon's older rooftops cluster around the Opera House. Saigon Saigon overlooks Lam Son Square, the Rex faces Nguyen Hue Boulevard, and walking down Dong Khoi towards the river you pass Hai Bar at the Sheraton (No. 88), Rooftop Grand Lounge at Hotel Grand Saigon (No. 8) and M Bar at the Majestic (No. 1); Song Bar at the Hilton on Me Linh Square is a few minutes away. Four of these have a band or live music in the evening and Song Bar hosts jazz three nights a week, so this is the area for anyone who prefers live musicians to a DJ. Beyond that cluster, Hai Ba Trung Street adds two more names: SolLuna at No. 65-67 in the old downtown core, and Papaya Papa up on floor 13 at No. 147, a stretch Google Maps places in the former District 3. Near Ben Thanh Market, Twilight on Ly Tu Trong looks straight down over the market, while Chill on Le Lai is about a kilometre away.

Opening times are spread across the whole day. Rooftop Grand Lounge serves from 6am, SolLuna from 7am, Blank from 9:30am, Song Bar from 2pm, Social Club and M Bar from 3pm; Zion, Banana Mama and Saigon Saigon from 4pm; Kén, Shri, Hai Bar, Twilight and Papaya Papa from 5pm; Chill from 5:30pm. For a late night, Zion runs to 3am every day; Papaya Papa goes on until 4am, but only on Friday and Saturday, the two nights Banana Mama also stays open to 3am. At the other end, SolLuna wraps up at 11pm and Shri at 11:30pm. Three places close on Mondays: Banana Mama, Kén and Papaya Papa. The order below follows Google Maps review count, highest first, rather than height, and the tips at the end cover timing, views, music and dress codes for each sky bar in Saigon. Every venue here is for guests aged 18 and over.

1. Chill Skybar — 26th floor of AB Tower on Le Lai, 360° views, DJs and dancers

  • AddressTầng 26, AB Tower, 76A Lê Lai, Phường Bến Thành, TP.HCM
  • Hours17:30 – 01:30
  • TypeSky Bar
  • Google Maps★ 4.4 · 6,032 reviews

Chill Skybar takes the whole open roof of the AB Tower at 76A Le Lai, beside the New World hotel, roughly one kilometre from Ben Thanh Market. The deck covers roughly 900 square metres with nothing blocking it, so wherever you stand you look down on the main downtown avenues. Doors open at 5:30pm and the night ends at 1:30am. One floor up is Chill Dining, a sister restaurant that grills beef and seafood over charcoal, useful if your group wants a full dinner before heading out onto the terrace.

Early in the evening it works as a lounge for watching the city switch on; once it is fully dark the DJ lifts the tempo and dancers join in, with a soundtrack of commercial Top 40, hip-hop and R&B. Seating splits into bar stools, standing tables and sofas. A tip from past guests: the terrace does not give a clean view of the sun going down, so Chill is at its best for the night skyline. Reservations mainly go to groups taking a table package, while couples usually walk in. The door is strict on dress: no shorts, flip-flops or tank tops.

Best for: Groups who want the whole city at night from high up, with DJs until well past midnight

2. Blank Sky Lounge — Floors 75–76 of Landmark 81, the highest here, open from 9:30am

  • AddressTầng 75–76, Landmark 81, 208 Nguyễn Hữu Cảnh, Phường Thạnh Mỹ Tây, TP.HCM
  • Hours09:30 – 00:00
  • TypeSky Lounge
  • Google Maps★ 4.5 · 2,958 reviews

Blank Sky Lounge spreads across floors 75 and 76 near the top of Landmark 81, 208 Nguyen Huu Canh, in Thanh My Tay Ward (the former Binh Thanh District). It bills itself as the highest café and bar lounge in Vietnam, roughly 461 metres up, which puts it more than thirty floors above the next-highest sky bar on this list. Lifts run up from the tower's main lobby, and there is parking in the building. It opens 9:30am to midnight every day, with the kitchen's last orders at 11:30pm; Google Maps shows an 11:30pm close, so check if you plan to stay late.

Unlike the open-air decks of District 1, most of Blank's seats sit behind floor-to-ceiling glass, with only a small outdoor terrace for a breath of high-altitude air. From up here the bends of the Saigon River and the whole downtown shrink to the scale of a map. The neutral interior by The Lab Saigon nods to Vietnam's rice-farming heritage; music stays at conversation level, with no DJ and no dance floor. Mornings bring coffee drinkers, and late afternoon is busiest because of the sunset. There is no dress code, smoking is banned, and tables are held for only 15 minutes, so be on time.

Best for: Anyone who wants Saigon from over 400 metres up, sunset dates, groups after a quiet place to talk

3. Social Club Rooftop Bar — Floor 24 of Hôtel des Arts, open from 3pm, Landmark 81 in view

  • AddressTầng 24, Hôtel des Arts Saigon, 76-78 Nguyễn Thị Minh Khai, Phường Xuân Hòa, TP.HCM
  • Hours15:00 – 00:00 · Fri, Sat until 01:00
  • TypeRooftop Bar
  • Google Maps★ 4.6 · 2,620 reviews

Social Club Rooftop Bar crowns Hôtel des Arts Saigon – MGallery from its 24th floor; the hotel stands at 76-78 Nguyen Thi Minh Khai in Xuan Hoa Ward, part of the former District 3. You reach it through the Social Club restaurant on floor 23 and a short flight of stairs. It opens at 3pm and closes at midnight, or at 1am on Friday and Saturday. The view spans about 270 degrees towards Landmark 81, and right beside the bar a glass sky bridge links the hotel to the Centec Tower.

The mood follows the hotel's French-Indochine theme: candles on the tables, armchairs for a quiet evening, standing room at the counter to watch the bartenders work, and outdoor tables for groups of friends. Music stays soft through the afternoon, some nights bring a live DJ, and Tuesday is Ladies' Night. The infinity pool next to the bar is for hotel guests only, so visitors just admire it. Dress is smart casual, and after 9pm nobody under 18 is admitted. Snacks come up from the kitchen below; the fried chicken bites and oysters get good words.

Best for: Couples, travellers who like a calm hotel terrace, anyone starting in mid-afternoon

4. Banana Mama Rooftop Bar & Kitchen — Floor 10 on Cong Quynh near Bui Vien, DJs Thu–Sun, shut Mondays

  • AddressTầng 10, 102 Cống Quỳnh, Phường Bến Thành, TP.HCM
  • Hours16:00 – 02:00 · Fri, Sat until 03:00 · closed Mon
  • TypeRooftop Bar
  • Google Maps★ 4.8 · 2,413 reviews

Banana Mama Rooftop Bar & Kitchen is on the 10th floor of 102 Cong Quynh in Ben Thanh Ward, a few minutes' walk from Bui Vien. The venue's own site calls the building the Mayfair hotel, while travel sites say WMC Tower; it is the same address. Open since 2019, it runs from 4pm to 2am on Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday and Sunday, until 3am on Friday and Saturday, and stays shut on Monday. At ten floors the view is close and warm, more streets and rooftops than a sweeping panorama.

Wooden decking, bright cushions and plenty of greenery give it the feel of a tropical beach club rather than a hushed hotel lounge. Sunset is the busy hour; resident DJs play Thursday to Sunday, and some evenings turn into sunset parties with confetti and sparklers. The kitchen is known for smash burgers made with grass-fed Australian beef, plus chicken and falafel options. From 7pm beachwear and flip-flops are turned away, so change your shoes if you are coming straight from Bui Vien. Weeknights you can walk in; at weekends, book ahead.

Best for: Young groups and Bui Vien visitors who want to open the night with a sunset and a burger

5. Shri Lifestyle Dining — Floor 23 of Centec Tower, five seating areas, DJs Friday–Saturday

  • AddressTầng 23, Centec Tower, 72-74 Nguyễn Thị Minh Khai, Phường Xuân Hòa, TP.HCM
  • Hours17:00 – 23:30
  • TypeRooftop Dining & Lounge
  • Google Maps★ 4.2 · 1,270 reviews

Shri Lifestyle Dining fills floor 23 of Centec Tower at 72-74 Nguyen Thi Minh Khai in Xuan Hoa Ward (the former District 3), the very building that the glass bridge beside Social Club's bar connects to, so the two rooftops are practically neighbours and easy to pair in one evening. Shri first opened in 2010 as a high-rise dinner spot; in 2023 it came under new management, was redesigned and reopened under its current name. It runs 5pm to 11:30pm daily, an earlier finish than most places here, and the edge of the roof gives a full circle of the city.

The floor is split into five areas; the best seats are the open-air terrace at sunset and the glass-roofed terrace wrapped in greenery, with a dining room and a lounge inside. Terracotta walls and plants give the whole level a Mediterranean-oasis feel. Music leans towards Melodic House as the light fades and shifts to Funky and Afro later on. DJs play on Friday and Saturday from 8:30pm, there is a weekly saxophone night out on the terrace, and Tuesday is Ladies Night. The kitchen does Asian-European fusion with tapas, sharing plates and set menus, ideal for a dinner date that turns into an evening of music.

Best for: Dinner dates high up, groups who like easy house music at weekends, pairing with Social Club

6. Twilight Sky Bar Sài Gòn — Floor 12 of La Siesta on Ly Tu Trong, 270° views, weekend EDM DJs

  • Address180-188 Lý Tự Trọng, Phường Bến Thành, TP.HCM
  • Hours17:00 – 00:00
  • TypeSky Bar
  • Google Maps★ 4.8 · 607 reviews

Twilight Sky Bar is up on the 12th floor of La Siesta Premium hotel at 180-188 Ly Tu Trong in Ben Thanh Ward; walk through the hotel lobby and take the lift. The terrace looks out across roughly 270 degrees over Ben Thanh Market and the park nearby, high enough to take in the whole neighbourhood yet low enough to pick out single rooftops and the flow of traffic. It runs 5pm to midnight every day with no closing day, which makes it a stop between sunset and midnight rather than a place to stay out until dawn.

The room is small and intimate, glass, metal and wood against black and stone grey, the kind of discreet hotel bar where chill-out music fills the weeknights. On Friday and Saturday a DJ plays EDM from 9pm to 11pm, and holidays bring special nights, such as 30 April 2026 with a DJ, dancers and fireworks seen from the 12th floor. The booking form takes groups of one to ten, and one regular says most of the crowd are international visitors, so it suits couples and small groups better than big parties. No dress code is published; neat clothes are enough.

Best for: Couples and small groups near Ben Thanh Market, anyone after a Friday or Saturday EDM set

7. Papaya Papa — Floor 13 on Hai Ba Trung, French-led kitchen, DJ parties to 4am

  • AddressTầng 13, 147 Hai Bà Trưng, Phường Xuân Hòa, TP.HCM
  • Hours17:00 – 02:00
  • TypeRooftop Restaurant, Bar & Club
  • Google Maps★ 4.6 · 553 reviews

Take the lift to floor 13 of the L'mak building, No. 147 Hai Ba Trung, and you are at Papaya Papa. Google Maps puts the address in Xuan Hoa Ward (the former District 3) while the venue's own site says District 1, so simply look for the street number and ride up to floor 13. It opens at 5pm from Tuesday to Sunday, closes at 2am on Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday and Sunday and stays open until 4am on Friday and Saturday, the latest finish in this guide on those two nights; Monday is its day off. According to The Dot Magazine, Banana Mama and Papaya Papa come from one team, but the Hai Ba Trung roof is quieter and aimed at an older crowd, so the two suit very different evenings.

Evenings move in three stages: golden hour as the sun drops behind the towers, a dinner cooked by a French chef who mixes classic French technique with Mediterranean touches and the lemongrass and citrus of Asian markets, then the party. Tuesday is Ladies Night; Wednesday, Thursday and Sunday pair dinner with a relaxed lounge; on Friday and Saturday DJs keep the floor going until close, and the venue promises no Vinahouse and no MC shouting into a microphone. Indoors there are sofas, potted plants and ceramics shelved behind the DJ booth, with an open-air section too; on sticky nights ask for a table inside. Smart casual applies: no shorts, sandals or singlets.

Best for: French-led dinner dates up high, groups who want a DJ party until 4am without Vinahouse

8. Rex Rooftop Garden Bar — The Rex Hotel's roof on Nguyen Hue, with a band in the evening

  • AddressTầng 5, Rex Hotel Saigon, 141 Nguyễn Huệ, Phường Sài Gòn, TP.HCM
  • TypeRooftop Bar
  • Google Maps★ 4 · 384 reviews

Rex Rooftop Garden Bar sits on the roof of the Rex Hotel at 141 Nguyen Hue in Saigon Ward; guides put it on floor 5, the lowest on this list. What it lacks in height it makes up for in position: from the railing you look straight down Nguyen Hue Boulevard, with the Opera House and City Hall only a few steps away. The bar is tied to the press history of the 1960s, when the US military's afternoon briefings were held in the hotel and foreign correspondents nicknamed them the “Five O'Clock Follies”. The hotel does not publish the bar's own hours; Wanderlog shows a 1am close, so check when booking.

The mood is that of a heritage hotel rather than a fashionable sky bar: open-air seating, the Rex's crown emblem and an evening cover band that plays fairly loud, so ask for a table away from the stage if you want to talk. Regulars love watching the French-era buildings around the square light up; the usual complaints are dated furniture for such a famous name and uneven food. On hot days the hotel's air-conditioned Vertical Garden is a quieter alternative. Aim to arrive around 5pm to see the square turn from daylight to lamplight.

Best for: Views down Nguyen Hue to City Hall, guests who enjoy a cover band in the evening

9. SolLuna Rooftop Bar — Poolside bar on floor 10 of Icon Saigon, open from 7am, chill music

  • Address65-67 Hai Bà Trưng, Phường Sài Gòn, TP.HCM
  • Hours07:00 – 23:00
  • TypeRooftop Pool Bar
  • Google Maps★ 4.8 · 365 reviews

SolLuna's bar sits at the edge of the pool ten floors up at the Icon Saigon hotel, No. 65-67 on Hai Ba Trung, right in the old downtown. It is the second-earliest opener in this guide at 7am, and the earliest to close: the venue's page says 11pm, while Google Maps shows 10:30pm, so check if you plan to arrive late. At ten floors, the same height as Banana Mama, the view is the District 1 skyline at close range rather than a distant panorama.

The setting feels more resort than nightlife: blue water, tropical plants, lounge sofas and bar stools ringed around the pool. SolLuna is not a club and posts no DJ schedule; the music is soft and chilled, and people come up for dates, after-work catch-ups or a rest after a day of sightseeing. The best hour is dusk, when the streets start to glow; the pool deck is open to the sky, so on rainy days ask about seating first. No dress code is published: relaxed in the daytime, a little neater in the evening.

Best for: Poolside lounging from morning to evening, quiet dates, friends meeting after work

10. M Bar Majestic Saigon — Floor 8 of the 1925 Majestic, river views, live band from 8pm

  • AddressTầng 8, Hotel Majestic Saigon, 1 Đồng Khởi, Phường Sài Gòn, TP.HCM
  • Hours15:00 – 00:00
  • TypeRooftop Bar
  • Google Maps★ 4.4 · 308 reviews

M Bar is on the 8th floor of the Majestic's New Wing, at the very start of Dong Khoi Street (No. 1), in a colonial-style hotel that has stood by the river since 1925 and turned 100 in 2025. Because it sits where Dong Khoi meets the waterfront, the terrace faces the Saigon River and Bach Dang wharf rather than the street. Hours are 3pm to midnight, seven days a week. The hotel also has Breeze Sky Bar on floor 5, a different venue that also faces the river and serves breakfast and grills, so ask specifically for M Bar on the eighth floor.

The bar was refurbished in 2018 but still feels like a hotel lounge: sit outside in the river breeze, watch the sun go down, then stay as local musicians take the stage at 8pm every night. There is no DJ or dance floor, and the style of music is not announced in advance. The light Western snacks get lukewarm reviews, so for a Vietnamese dinner try Saigon House on floor 7 before heading up. The best plan: arrive between 5pm and 6pm for sunset on the water and stay past 8pm for the band.

Best for: Couples who like a century-old hotel setting, sunset over the river followed by a live band

11. Zion Sky Lounge & Dining — Floor 14 on Ham Nghi, dinner with a skyline view, open to 3am daily

  • AddressTầng 14, 87A Hàm Nghi, Phường Nguyễn Thái Bình, Quận 1, TP.HCM
  • Hours16:00 – 03:00
  • TypeSky Lounge & Dining
  • Google Maps★ 4.7 · 224 reviews

Zion Sky Lounge & Dining is on the 14th floor of 87A Ham Nghi in Nguyen Thai Binh Ward, a few minutes on foot from Ben Thanh metro station; in the lobby, mention Zion at reception and take the lift. It is the only rooftop here that runs to 3am every single night: 4pm to 3am, Mondays included. From this height most of the downtown core is laid out in front of you, the towers along Ham Nghi lighting up one after another, and the colours peak around 6pm. It holds about 300 guests, so larger groups are easy to seat.

More than a bar, Zion runs as a proper restaurant: sharing plates from the afternoon, then a late menu of small dishes from 10pm for those staying on. After dark, singers and weekly guest DJs move the music from Top 40 towards EDM, House, Hip-hop and Techno, and the Tuesday Art Attack night adds fire dancing and body painting. Seating comes in five tiers, ranging from counter seats for anyone alone up to VIP sofas that hold fifteen on Friday and Saturday. Dress is smart elegant, the strictest on this list.

Best for: Client dinners, dates and groups who want to eat first, then stay for the music

12. Saigon Saigon Rooftop Bar — Ninth floor of the Caravelle on Lam Son, live band, reopened 2026

  • AddressTầng 9, Caravelle Saigon, 19-23 Công trường Lam Sơn, Phường Sài Gòn, TP.HCM
  • Hours16:00 – 01:00
  • TypeRooftop Bar
  • Google Maps★ 4 · 171 reviews

Saigon Saigon Rooftop Bar belongs to the Caravelle hotel at 19-23 Lam Son Square, by the Opera House at the top of Dong Khoi. Most sources say floor 9 while the hotel's dining page says floor 10, so just give the bar's name at the lift. Few rooftops downtown have as long a history: it was a meeting place for Saigon society in the 1950s and 1960s, then the spot where foreign correspondents followed the news during the war. After a renovation it reopened on 28 September 2026 and now runs from 4pm to 1am.

The new look mixes disco, Art Deco geometry and old Saigon architecture: checkerboard floor tiles, louvred shutters, red glazed tiles, a bar in the middle of the room and a vintage typewriter on display beside archive photos. From 5:45pm to 7:45pm a bartender wheels a trolley up to the tables while a DJ plays, and from 8pm a live band takes over until 10:30pm, or 10pm on Sunday. Floor 9 is well below the true sky bars, but you look straight down onto the square. There is no air conditioning, so dress light and neat on hot evenings.

Best for: History lovers and groups who want a live band right in the city centre

13. Song Bar Hilton Saigon — Top floor (40) of the Hilton by the river, glass walls, jazz nights

  • AddressTầng 40, Hilton Saigon, 11 Công trường Mê Linh, Phường Sài Gòn, TP.HCM
  • Hours14:00 – 00:00
  • TypeSky Bar
  • Google Maps★ 4.6 · 141 reviews

Song Bar occupies the 40th and highest floor of Hilton Saigon on Me Linh Square (No. 11) in Saigon Ward, a short walk from the riverbank. It opened in 2024 alongside Residence Eleven, the hotel's Chinese restaurant one floor down, and it is the second-highest sky bar in this guide after Blank. Glass runs from floor to ceiling all the way round, so even in the rain you can see the river and the downtown skyline. It opens 2pm to midnight daily; after a maintenance break between 22 September and 1 October 2026, it reopened on 2 October.

Inside it is warmer and more enclosed than the open-air decks: wood panelling, velvet seats, sculptural lamps and a few East Asian touches. Afternoons bring a seasonal afternoon tea; on Wednesday, Friday and Saturday from 9pm, Jazz Night brings live vocals and the singers take requests. Early evening tends to be quiet, which is the easiest time to get a seat by the glass as the sky changes colour. For dinner first, Residence Eleven downstairs serves Peking duck, dim sum and dishes from a wood-fired oven.

Best for: Couples and small groups who like jazz, anyone wanting a covered seat with a river view

14. Kén Skybar — Open-air terrace on floor 26 by the Saigon River, closed Mondays

  • AddressTầng 26, Riverfront Financial Centre, 3A-3B Tôn Đức Thắng, Phường Sài Gòn, TP.HCM
  • Hours17:00 – 02:00 · Tue – Sun
  • TypeSkybar
  • Google Maps★ 4.7 · 118 reviews

Kén Skybar occupies level 26 of the Riverfront Financial Centre, the tower at 3A-3B Ton Duc Thang in Saigon Ward, beside the Ba Son area and Bach Dang wharf. It matches Chill for height but faces the other way: ahead of you is a bend of the Saigon River with lights along both banks, and overhead there is no roof at all. Tables are turned towards the glass railing, so almost every seat has the water in view. Hours are 5pm to 2am, Tuesday to Sunday; it is closed on Monday.

The look is a cream butterfly on deep plum, a curved steel canopy over the central bar that recalls the threads of a cocoon, and banana plants and palms lit green from below. Tuesday is Ladies Night, and on Friday and Saturday the Weekend Line Up has DJs moving between deep house, Afro house and R&B, with V-pop remixes later on. Riverside tables seat two to six by the glass, and VIP sofas take six to twelve. Dress is smart casual. The lifts get busy at weekends, so arrive about half an hour before sunset.

Best for: Dates at dusk over the river, groups who like deep house under an open sky

15. Rooftop Grand Lounge — Floor 20 of Hotel Grand Saigon on Dong Khoi, live music Thu–Sun

  • AddressTầng 20, Hotel Grand Saigon, 8 Đồng Khởi, Phường Sài Gòn, TP.HCM
  • Hours06:00 – 00:00
  • TypeRooftop Lounge
  • Google Maps★ 4.3 · 104 reviews

Rooftop Grand Lounge is on floor 20 of Hotel Grand Saigon at No. 8 Dong Khoi, Saigon Ward, on the stretch closest to the river. The hotel is more than 95 years old, one of the city's longest-standing heritage hotels, and the roof is high enough to take in both the Dong Khoi quarter and the water. It opens earliest of anywhere here, running straight through from 6am to midnight every day and starting with breakfast; Wanderlog shows an 11:30pm close, so check if you plan to stay late. There are both covered and open-air tables, which makes rainy evenings easier.

By day it works as a restaurant serving Western, Asian and Vietnamese dishes, plus afternoon tea from 1pm to 6pm in an Asian set and a European set. From Thursday to Sunday, live music plays from 8pm to 10pm, gentle enough for conversation rather than dancing. Guests often order the egg coffee; recurring complaints are some bland Western dishes and slow service for large groups. For sunset and music in one visit, reserve a seat outside for a Thursday, Friday, Saturday or Sunday night and be there before 6pm.

Best for: Friends meeting for afternoon tea, couples after soft live music with a river view at weekends

16. Hai Bar Sheraton Saigon — Floor 23 of the Sheraton on Dong Khoi, opened June 2026, Opera views

  • AddressTầng 23, Sheraton Saigon Grand Opera, 88 Đồng Khởi, Phường Sài Gòn, TP.HCM
  • Hours17:00 – 01:00
  • TypeRooftop Bar
  • Google Maps★ 4.9 · 29 reviews

Hai Bar is one of the newest names in this guide: an open-air bar twenty-three floors up at the Sheraton Saigon Grand Opera, No. 88 Dong Khoi, launched on 26 June 2026 when the hotel rebuilt the whole floor to mark 23 years in business. The name puns on “hai, ba”, Vietnamese for two-three, and “high bar”, and the hotel says no rooftop on Dong Khoi sits higher. From the railing you see the Opera House façade and the twin spires of Notre-Dame Cathedral, with the Saigon River and Ba Son Bridge further off. The hotel lists hours of 5pm to 1am daily, which may change with the season.

The same floor holds Altitude 23, a double-height round atrium with a sculptural chandelier on the site of the old Night Spot club, plus the Summit meeting rooms facing Nguyen Hue, so Hai Bar often serves as a meeting point before a party or conference. The signature menu is built around Vietnam's three regions, northern spices, central herbs and southern fruit, with light bites on the side. As for music, the hotel mentions an evening programme but has not posted genres or a DJ schedule. Dress is smart casual, with no beachwear or flip-flops; at weekends, go up right at 5pm to get a railing seat.

Best for: Anyone curious about Dong Khoi's newest rooftop, groups meeting before a party or conference

How to choose

Choose by height and view

By height: Blank on floors 75–76, Song Bar on 40, Chill and Kén on 26, Social Club on 24, Shri and Hai Bar on 23, Rooftop Grand Lounge on 20, Zion on 14, Papaya Papa on 13, Twilight on 12, Banana Mama and SolLuna on 10, Saigon Saigon on 9, M Bar on 8 and the Rex on 5. Higher is not automatically better. Six venues give you the river: Kén is open-air right above the water, Song Bar and Blank see it through glass, M Bar looks down on Bach Dang wharf, Grand faces the water from the end of Dong Khoi and Hai Bar adds Ba Son Bridge. For a full circle of the city, pick Chill or Shri; Social Club looks out towards Landmark 81, Zion frames the downtown towers as they light up, and Twilight looks down over Ben Thanh Market. The Rex, Saigon Saigon, Banana Mama and SolLuna are low enough to see street life, rooftops and the square below, which makes for photos with depth rather than a distant panorama; at SolLuna the pool adds a foreground.

Sunset, live music or a DJ

The sky over Saigon usually changes colour between about 5:30pm and 7pm. To pick a seat without rushing, head for an early opener: Grand from 6am, SolLuna from 7am, Blank from 9:30am, Song Bar from 2pm, Social Club and M Bar from 3pm, then Zion, Banana Mama and Saigon Saigon from 4pm. Kén, Shri, Hai Bar, Twilight and Papaya Papa open at 5pm, so go up as the doors open; Chill only opens at 5:30pm and its terrace misses the actual sunset, so save it for the city lights. After dark, choose by sound: live bands at Saigon Saigon, M Bar, the Rex and Grand (Thursday to Sunday); jazz at Song Bar on Wednesday, Friday and Saturday; a weekly saxophone night at Shri; DJs with more energy at Chill, Kén, Banana Mama, Zion or Shri at weekends; an EDM set at Twilight from 9pm to 11pm on Friday and Saturday; and a DJ party at Papaya Papa until 4am on those same nights. Blank and SolLuna keep the music in the background.

Mondays, rain and closing times

Banana Mama, Kén and Papaya Papa are closed on Monday; the rest open as usual, though the Rex does not publish a schedule, so ask first. In the rainy season Kén and Hai Bar are open to the sky, Banana Mama sits on an outdoor deck and SolLuna's pool deck is uncovered, so ask about seating when our team calls to confirm; Blank and Song Bar are almost entirely behind glass, Grand and Shri have covered sections and Papaya Papa has an indoor room, which makes them good back-ups. Saigon Saigon has no air conditioning and can feel warm before dark. Closing times: Papaya Papa 4am on Friday and Saturday (2am other nights), Zion 3am every night, Banana Mama 3am on Friday and Saturday, Kén 2am, Chill 1:30am, Hai Bar and Saigon Saigon 1am, Social Club midnight (1am at weekends), Blank, Song Bar, M Bar, Grand and Twilight midnight, Shri 11:30pm and SolLuna 11pm. Plan your last stop around these so you are not asked to leave halfway through.

Chill refuses shorts, flip-flops and tank tops and checks at the door. Blank has no dress code but bans smoking. Hotel rooftops such as the Rex, M Bar, Grand, Song Bar, Saigon Saigon, Twilight and SolLuna publish no rule, so neat clothes are enough. Frequently asked questions

What is the highest rooftop bar in Saigon?

The highest is Blank Sky Lounge, on floors 75 and 76 of Landmark 81 in Binh Thanh, which calls itself the highest café and bar lounge in Vietnam. In the old downtown, the highest is Song Bar on the 40th floor of the Hilton on Me Linh Square, followed by Chill Skybar and Kén Skybar on floor 26, Social Club on 24, and Shri and Hai Bar on 23. On Dong Khoi Street itself, the Sheraton says Hai Bar is the highest rooftop.

Which rooftop bar in Ho Chi Minh City stays open latest?

Taken night by night, Zion Sky Lounge & Dining is the latest, open until 3am every night including Monday. On Friday and Saturday only, Papaya Papa keeps its DJ party going until 4am and Banana Mama runs to 3am; on other nights both close at 2am. Next come Kén at 2am, Chill at 1:30am, and Hai Bar and Saigon Saigon at 1am. Most hotel rooftops, including Song Bar, M Bar, Rooftop Grand Lounge, Blank and Twilight, close at midnight, as does Social Club (1am on Friday and Saturday), while Shri closes at 11:30pm and SolLuna at 11pm.

Which Saigon rooftop bars are open on Monday?

Banana Mama, Kén Skybar and Papaya Papa close on Monday, reopening on Tuesday. Chill, Blank, Social Club, Shri, Twilight, SolLuna, M Bar, Zion, Saigon Saigon, Song Bar, Rooftop Grand Lounge and Hai Bar all open; the Rex does not publish the bar's schedule, so ask when booking. For a late Monday, Zion is the safest choice because it runs to 3am.

Where can I watch the sunset from a rooftop in Saigon?

To claim a seat before the sky turns, pick an early opener: SolLuna from 7am, with the pool in the foreground; Blank from 9:30am, with the sunset seen from floor 75; Song Bar from 2pm behind the glass on floor 40; Social Club from 3pm, facing Landmark 81; and M Bar, also from 3pm, where the sun sets over the river. Banana Mama builds its evenings around sunset, Papaya Papa opens every night with a golden hour on its 13th-floor roof, and Zion peaks around 6pm over the Ham Nghi towers. Kén and Hai Bar offer open-air sunsets and Twilight watches dusk settle over Ben Thanh Market, but all three only open at 5pm, so go up early. Chill is better after dark, since its terrace misses the sun going down.

Which rooftop bars in Saigon have a river view?

There are six. Kén Skybar on floor 26 on Ton Duc Thang is open-air and faces the water directly; ask for a Riverside table by the glass, which seats two to six. Song Bar on the 40th floor of the Hilton sees the river through glass. M Bar on floor 8 of the Majestic and Rooftop Grand Lounge on floor 20 sit at the river end of Dong Khoi. Hai Bar on floor 23 of the Sheraton adds Ba Son Bridge, and Blank shows the whole curve of the river around the city centre from floor 75.

Is there a rooftop bar with a pool in Saigon?

Yes. SolLuna is a bar set right beside the pool on the 10th floor of the Icon Saigon hotel at 65-67 Hai Ba Trung, open 7am to 11pm, with sofas and bar stools around the water. Social Club on the 24th floor of Hôtel des Arts also has an infinity pool next to the bar, but it is reserved for hotel guests, so visitors can only admire it. On rainy days, check seating at SolLuna first, as the pool deck is open to the sky.

Which rooftop bars in Ho Chi Minh City have live music or jazz?

Four hotel rooftops around the centre have live bands: Saigon Saigon from 8pm (finishing earlier on Sunday), M Bar every night from 8pm, the Rex in the evening, and Rooftop Grand Lounge from Thursday to Sunday, 8pm to 10pm. Song Bar holds Jazz Night on Wednesday, Friday and Saturday from 9pm, and the singers take requests. Shri has a weekly saxophone night on its terrace, and at Zion singers perform before the DJs take over.

Which hotel rooftops are near the Saigon Opera House?

Five hotel rooftops sit close together: Saigon Saigon at the Caravelle on Lam Son Square, Rex Rooftop Garden Bar at the Rex facing Nguyen Hue, then along Dong Khoi Street Hai Bar at the Sheraton (No. 88), Rooftop Grand Lounge at Hotel Grand Saigon (No. 8) and M Bar at the Majestic (No. 1). The Majestic opened in 1925, the Grand is more than 95 years old, and both the Rex and the Caravelle are tied to the foreign press of the war years. You can walk down Dong Khoi from the Opera House to the river and visit two or three in one evening; Song Bar at the Hilton is only a few minutes away.

Which rooftop bars are near Ben Thanh Market?

The closest is Twilight Sky Bar on the 12th floor of La Siesta hotel, 180-188 Ly Tu Trong, which looks straight down over the market and the park beside it, open 5pm to midnight. Chill Skybar, on the 26th floor of the AB Tower at 76A Le Lai, is about a kilometre from the market and stays open until 1:30am. Banana Mama at 102 Cong Quynh is in the same Ben Thanh Ward near Bui Vien, and Zion on floor 14 of Ham Nghi is a few minutes' walk from Ben Thanh metro station.

Do I need to book a rooftop bar in Saigon?

On weeknights you can usually walk in, especially at Banana Mama and the hotel rooftops in the afternoon. For Friday, Saturday or the sunset hour, book ahead, because railing and window seats go first; Chill keeps reservations mainly for table packages, and Blank holds a table for only 15 minutes; Twilight's booking form only takes groups of one to ten, so larger parties should look at somewhere roomier such as Zion. Booking through Nightclub.com.vn is free, and our team contacts the venue to confirm before you arrive.

What should I wear to a rooftop bar in Saigon?

Smart casual is the safe level. Zion asks for smart elegant; Chill refuses shorts, flip-flops and tank tops, and Papaya Papa likewise rules out shorts, sandals and singlets; Banana Mama refuses beachwear and flip-flops from 7pm, and Hai Bar asks guests to avoid both. Blank has no dress code, and at SolLuna relaxed daytime clothes are fine, a little neater after dark. It gets windy up high late at night, so bring a light layer, and at Saigon Saigon, which has no air conditioning, wear something breathable.
