Tòa nhà Somerset Vista

Tòa nhà Somerset Vista is a building in Quận Hai, Ho Chi Minh City. Tòa nhà Somerset Vista is situated near the place of worship St. Michael the Archangel Church, as well as near the Buddhist temple Pháp Viện Minh Đăng Quang.
