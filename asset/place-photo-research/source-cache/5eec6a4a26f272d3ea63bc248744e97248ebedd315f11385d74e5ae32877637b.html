Chợ Tân Sơn Nhất

Chợ Tân Sơn Nhất is a building in Ho Chi Minh City, Southern Vietnam, which is located on Nguyễn Thái Sơn. Chợ Tân Sơn Nhất is situated near the police station Chốt Công An Phường 2, Quận Tân Bình, as well as near the carousel Rạp Xiếc Thành Phố Hồ Chí Minh.
