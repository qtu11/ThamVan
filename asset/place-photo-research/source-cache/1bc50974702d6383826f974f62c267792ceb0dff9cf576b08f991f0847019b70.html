Giáo xứ Bình Lợi

Giáo xứ Bình Lợi is a Roman Catholic church in Ho Chi Minh City, Southern Vietnam, which is located on Bình Lợi. Giáo xứ Bình Lợi is situated near the church Nhà Thờ Bình Lợi, as well as near the pitch Pickleball 426.
