Trường Trung học cơ sở Nguyễn Gia Thiều

Trường Trung học cơ sở Nguyễn Gia Thiều is a school in Quận Bình Tân, Ho Chi Minh City, which is located on Đường Lý Thường Kiệt. Trường Trung học cơ sở Nguyễn Gia Thiều is situated near the police station Tan Hoa Ward Police, as well as near the town hall People’s Committee of Tan Hoa ward.
