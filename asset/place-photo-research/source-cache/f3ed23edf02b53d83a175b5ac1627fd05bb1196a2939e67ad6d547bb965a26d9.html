Jerrico Lounge

Jerrico Lounge is a bar in District 1, Ho Chi Minh City, which is located on Cao Bá Nhạ. Jerrico Lounge is situated near Công Ty TNHH Du Lịch Việt Today, as well as near the place of worship Nam Thành Holy See.
