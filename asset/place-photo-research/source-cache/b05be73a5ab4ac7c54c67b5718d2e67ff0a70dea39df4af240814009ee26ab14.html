Bai Dau Church

Bai Dau Church is a Roman Catholic church in Vung Tau, Ho Chi Minh City, which is located on Trần Phú. Bai Dau Church is situated near Bai Dau Pilgrimage Centre, as well as near the church Chapel of Vietnamese martyrs.
