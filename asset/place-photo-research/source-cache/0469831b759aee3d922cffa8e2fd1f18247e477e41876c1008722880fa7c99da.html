Đỉnh Hàm Rồng

Đỉnh Hàm Rồng is a scenic viewpoint in Đất Đỏ District, Ho Chi Minh City. Đỉnh Hàm Rồng is situated near the peak Núi Châu Viên, as well as near the cave Hang Dơi.
