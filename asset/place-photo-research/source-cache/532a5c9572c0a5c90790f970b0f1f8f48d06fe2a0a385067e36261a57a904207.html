Chợ Bình Thới

Chợ Bình Thới is a marketplace in Ho Chi Minh City, Southern Vietnam, which is located on Đường Xóm Đất. Chợ Bình Thới is situated near the government office UBND phường 2, as well as near UBND phường 10.
