Trung Tâm Tiếng Anh Yola

Trung Tâm Tiếng Anh Yola is a school in Phường Bình Trị Đông B, Quận Bình Tân, Ho Chi Minh City, which is located on Đường số 17A. Trung Tâm Tiếng Anh Yola is situated near the amusement arcade Dream Games, as well as near the movie theater CGV Cinema.
