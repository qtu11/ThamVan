COFFEE BEAN & TEA LEAFS

COFFEE BEAN & TEA LEAFS is a café in District 1, Ho Chi Minh City, which is located on Đường Trần Hưng Ðạo. COFFEE BEAN & TEA LEAFS is situated near Saigon Evangelical Church, as well as near the theater building Nhà Hát ‘Hàng Không Mẫu Hạm’.
