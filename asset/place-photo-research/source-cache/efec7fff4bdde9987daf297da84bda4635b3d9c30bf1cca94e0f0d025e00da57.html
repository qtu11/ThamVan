Pizza Hut

Pizza Hut is a restaurant in Quận Thủ Đức, Ho Chi Minh City, which is located on Võ Văn Ngân. Pizza Hut is situated near Thu Duc Catholic Parish Church, as well as near the marketplace Chợ Thủ Đức B.
