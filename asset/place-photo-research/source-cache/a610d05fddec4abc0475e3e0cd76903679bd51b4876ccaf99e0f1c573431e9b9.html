Chanchamayo Coffee

Chanchamayo Coffee is a café in Ho Chi Minh City, Southern Vietnam, which is located on Ngô Thời Nhiệm. Chanchamayo Coffee is situated near the marketplace Phu Nhuan Market, as well as near the church Giáo xứ Công Lý.
