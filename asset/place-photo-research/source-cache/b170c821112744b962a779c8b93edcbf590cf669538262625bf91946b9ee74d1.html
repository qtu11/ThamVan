Ba Ria Vung Tau University

Ba Ria Vung Tau University is a university in Phường Bảy, Vung Tau, Ho Chi Minh City, which is located on Trương Văn Bang. Ba Ria Vung Tau University is situated near the police station Police of Tam Thắng Ward, as well as near the stadium Nhà thi đấu Đa năng.
