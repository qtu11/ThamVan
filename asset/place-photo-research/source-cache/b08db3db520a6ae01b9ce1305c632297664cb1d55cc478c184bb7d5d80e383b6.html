Trường Trung học cơ sở Quang Trung

Trường Trung học cơ sở Quang Trung is a school building in Ho Chi Minh City, Southern Vietnam, which is located on Đường Quang Trung. Trường Trung học cơ sở Quang Trung is situated near the public building People’s Committee of Go Vap ward, as well as near the church Nhà Thờ Xóm Thuốc.
