Cửa Hàng BMW Motorrad Sala

Cửa Hàng BMW Motorrad Sala is a motorcycle shop in Quận Hai, Ho Chi Minh City, which is located on Mai Chí Thọ. Cửa Hàng BMW Motorrad Sala is situated near the bridge Cầu Kênh 2, as well as near the shopping center SALA Shopping Mall Thiso mall.
